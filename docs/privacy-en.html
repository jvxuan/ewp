PEW Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-28 ｜ Provider: PEW (individual developer)

This policy describes how PEW ("the App") handles information when you use it. PEW is a local-first to-do and notes tool designed to collect as little personal information as possible. Please read this policy before using the App.

1. Information we collect and store

  • The App does not require an account and does not collect your name, phone number, ID number, or other identity information.
  • Items you enter — to-dos, notes, work records, remarks and categories — are stored only in the local storage (localStorage) of your current device and are not uploaded to a server by default.
  • The App does not read your device contacts, photos, files, location, microphone, or device identifiers.

2. Permissions requested

  • Internet access: used to load the App's own page content.
  • The App does not request location, camera, photo/storage read, contacts, microphone, sensor, or push notification permissions.

3. Third-party services and external links

The App itself does not integrate analytics, push, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

The sponsor page may include links to third-party platforms (such as PayPal.me and Ko-fi). After tapping such a link you leave the App and enter the third-party page, whose information practices are governed by that third party and its own privacy policy.

4. Backup, export and deletion

  • You can export all local records to a JSON file for your own backup, and restore them by importing a backup file.
  • You can also send the backup file to your own email via your mail client; the App does not send mail on your behalf and does not keep a copy of the backup.
  • Deleted records first move to the trash, where they can be restored or permanently removed. Uninstalling the App removes the local data saved on the device.
  • Because data is kept on the device, switching devices or clearing App data requires restoring from a backup you exported earlier.

5. Minors

The App does not offer social, messaging, public posting, or services aimed specifically at minors. Minors should use the App under the guidance of a guardian, who can manage the records and backups on the device.

6. Policy updates

We may update this policy as features change. The updated policy will be published on this page; material changes will be noticed in the App where appropriate. Please check this page periodically for the latest version.
